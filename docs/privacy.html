Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

DataDroid is an on-device app for building image datasets. It is designed so your images never leave your device unless you choose to share them.

What DataDroid stores

Datasets, class names, images, thumbnails, image embeddings used by auto-sort, and your settings are saved in the app's private storage on your device. Deleting a dataset, or uninstalling the app, removes them.

What DataDroid sends

Nothing. DataDroid has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not upload your images or any other data. The auto-sort model is bundled with the app and runs entirely on your device.

Permissions

  • Camera: used only to capture images for your datasets, and only while the camera view is open.
  • Photos and files: DataDroid uses the system pickers, so it can read only the photos, folders or zip files you choose.

Sharing

When you export a dataset, DataDroid creates a zip and opens the system share sheet. Where the zip goes from there is up to you and the app you choose.

Children

DataDroid does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions are welcome at tawhidmonowar@duck.com or through GitHub issues.
